# Research protocol

Frozen 2026-11-18. Use two funds, one dealer, one outside investor and one repo lender, with one synthetic security initially at $1. Funds hold 100 and 80 units and finance inventory with repo liabilities. Set initial leverage to 3, 5, 8, 12 or 16 in both funds. Initial advance rate is 1 − 1/(1.3×leverage). A common shock s reprices the asset by 0.25s and reduces the advance rate by 0.5s. Draw s=0.20×Beta(2,5) with seed 8808. Compare no forced sales, forced sales with unlimited dealer inventory capacity, and capacity of 0, 15, 30, 60 or 120 units.

After the shock, mark collateral and call margin. Compute orders using current collateral and assume sale proceeds repay repo debt. Allocate to the dealer first, with capacity and cash constraints, and the remainder to the outside investor. Each batch clears at the prior mark times exp(−impact × outside purchases / initial inventory), floored at $0.25. Settle cash and securities, repay repo, revalue, and repeat. Baseline impact parameter is 0.16. A cascade means at least five units sold after the first round. Stop when margin-compliant, no inventory can be sold, or 100 rounds elapse. Record remaining violations and termination reasons. Counterparties start with sufficient cash for the modeled inventory and all transactions occur inside the ledger.

Use the same 400 shock draws across model variants, leverage and capacity. Report cascade fraction and binomial standard error, second-round sales and peak impact relative to the post-shock initial mark. Compare batch clearing to reversed sequential order, halve/double impact and vary allocation of unchanged aggregate holdings. These are simulation scenarios. No calibration or holdout split applies. Verify no-shock, unlimited capacity, hand-calculated first round, cash/inventory conservation and stopping. Replay the fixed s=0.08, 8× leverage, 30-unit capacity case and show its per-agent balances.
